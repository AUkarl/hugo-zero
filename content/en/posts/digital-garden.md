## What Is a Digital Garden

A digital garden is a different way of organizing content from traditional blogging. Instead of a reverse-chronological list of articles, it's an organically growing knowledge network.

## Principles of Cultivation

### 1. Keep Tending
Don't expect to plant once and harvest. Come back regularly to tend your ideas and let them evolve over time.

### 2. Make Connections
Let ideas connect to each other, forming a knowledge network. A mature idea links to multiple related concepts.

### 3. Grow in Public
Don't wait until things are perfect before sharing. Let ideas grow in the open, gathering feedback and inspiration.

## My Practice

When I started building this site with Hugo, I treated it as a digital garden. There are incomplete thoughts, developing viewpoints, and polished articles alike.

## The Harvest Season

After consistent cultivation, you'll be pleasantly surprised to find:

- Connections between ideas grow richer
- New ideas take root more easily
- You can clearly see your thinking evolve over time

## Summary

A digital garden is a long-term content strategy. It doesn't chase short-term traffic or trends, but focuses on depth and connection of ideas.
